Features

What it does

Status Stages
Wishlist, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Rejected, or Ghosted — color-coded and editable inline.
Status History
Every status change is timestamped automatically, powering the analytics view.
Search & Filter
Search by company, role, or notes, and filter by work mode or status stage.
Sankey Funnel
A flow diagram visualizing exactly where applications move — and where they drop off.
Export / Import
Download your data as JSON and import it on another device — no account syncing needed.
Responsive
Built to work cleanly on both desktop and mobile, including a scrollable table view.

Data Storage

Where your data lives

Everything is saved locally in your browser's storage — no account, no cloud, no database. Nothing is ever sent to a server.

# Storage
window.localStorage   → browser-native, per-device storage

# Key
jobTrackerRows     → every application, status, and history entry
